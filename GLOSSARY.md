# Rock Science

The language of Rock Science, Soheil's one-person product studio, and of rockscience.com, the site that sells its services.

## Language

### The studio

**Rock Science**:
Soheil's solo product studio in Germany, presented openly as one person.
_Avoid_: agency, company, team

**Thesis**:
The studio's design law: apps should be intuitive, know what the user wants and needs, and carry nothing that gets in the way of that.
_Avoid_: minimalism, "simple" (when it implies plain-looking)

### What the studio makes

**Product**:
An app Rock Science owns and operates itself: tabley or thenix.
_Avoid_: project, client work

**tabley**:
The studio's restaurant and café reservation Product, live with restaurants that use it.

**thenix**:
The studio's evidence-based training planner for calisthenics and running; public, but early.
_Avoid_: calisthenics app

**Tooling**:
Open-source software Soheil builds to make his own building faster and better: Smith and matrix. Tooling is not a Product.
_Avoid_: product, side project

**Smith**:
The Tooling that generates a new project on the studio's chosen stack, ready for AI agents from the start.

**matrix**:
The Tooling that runs an agent loop in sandboxes, working through dependent tickets with automated checks and code review.

### Who the studio serves

**Client**:
A business that pays Rock Science for an end-to-end product build or for contract engineering.
_Avoid_: customer (reserve for a Product's users)

**Guest**:
A person who books a table through tabley.
_Avoid_: customer, user
